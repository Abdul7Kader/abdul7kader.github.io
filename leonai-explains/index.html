LeonAI Upload

Private publishing tool for the YouTube channel “LeonAI Explains”

LeonAI Upload is a small private tool that the owner of the YouTube channel LeonAI Explains uses on their own computer. It uploads the channel’s short explainer videos and sets their titles, descriptions and tags, and it reads the channel’s own statistics (views, watch time) to improve future videos.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users. It only accesses the YouTube channel of its owner, after the owner signs in with Google and grants access.

Google user data

How the tool uses data from Google accounts is described in the Privacy Policy. Use of the tool is subject to the Terms of Service.
