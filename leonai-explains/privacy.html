Privacy Policy

LeonAI Upload · Last updated: 21 October 2026

LeonAI Upload (“the tool”) is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel “LeonAI Explains” to publish and manage videos on that channel.

Data the tool accesses

With the owner’s permission, through the YouTube Data API: the owner’s own channel, videos, titles, descriptions, tags and channel statistics. The tool does not access data of any other person.

How the data is used

Only to upload and update videos on the owner’s channel and to read that channel’s statistics. The tool’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and statistics are stored only locally on the owner’s computer. No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and no data is used for advertising.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless; the owner can delete all locally stored data at any time.
